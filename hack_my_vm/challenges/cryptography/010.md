# Concept 
Understanding to Identify Base85 or Ascii Encoding and how to decode or decrypt it 

# Method of Solve
- Go to the challenge URL
- copy the challenge encrypted text and visit the cyberchef.io
- Since it is combination of numbers, special characters and alphanumeric it is probably Base85 encrypted
- Decrypt it using From Base85
- The flag is **HMV{wrtzxcvfdghyt}**
